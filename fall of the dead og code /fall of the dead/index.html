You were Sir Aldric of Varrow, a knight of the Order of Saint Anselm. In eleven years you never missed a Mass.

Then came the plague summer. You buried your wife and your son in the same week, and the priest would not come to the graves for fear of catching it.

You threw your sword and your mail into the river, and you drank for three years.

Tonight the bells of Varrow are ringing and nobody is pulling the ropes. The dead are in the streets.

No one is coming to save this town. There is only you, and you are drunk.
